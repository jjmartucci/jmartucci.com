Being in tech a lot of the information about how things went wrong was not new to me, but it’s a detailed account of who and what made the current Internet so shitty. I think it’s a bit more optimistic about the future than I am, and avoids the topic of today where we’re condensing the shitty-net into like 3 giant AI companies. But in doing my part, I bought the book at the local book store, I’ll donate it to the library, this review is on Hardcover not Goodreads, none of it was written by AI, fuck Jeff Bezos.
